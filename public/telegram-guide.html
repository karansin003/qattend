1. How Telegram Linking Works

QAttend uses a secure deep-linking protocol through the official Telegram Bot API. Instead of asking you for your Telegram phone number, QAttend generates a unique, cryptographically random linkage token:

  1. In your QAttend dashboard, click Connect Telegram.
  2. A button opens Telegram directly to the official QAttend bot with your unique start payload: https://t.me/<bot>?start=<code>.
  3. Pressing Start in Telegram verifies the token on the server and permanently binds that Telegram chat ID to your student account.
  4. Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation: One Telegram chat can belong to exactly one QAttend account. Notifications are addressed to you by your official student name (never your email), and data is never cross-delivered between students.

2. Verified Notification Types & Exact Schedules

Every notification delivered by QAttend corresponds to an active background process verified in the application code:

🌅 8:30 AM IST — Daily Class Timetable

Cron: 30 8 * * *

Every morning at 8:30 AM IST, QAttend delivers your complete lecture schedule for the day before first period starts. Each entry specifies the period timing, course code, subject title, room number, and assigned faculty member.

Resilience: Includes automatic missed-run recovery if the server was temporarily sleeping, with an 11:00 AM IST cutoff to avoid delivering outdated morning messages.

⚡ Fast Loop — Real-Time Attendance Marking

Interval: Every 5 Mins

During college hours (08:30 to 17:00 IST), QAttend monitors your today's attendance table every 5 minutes. The moment a professor transitions a period from "N.M." (Not Marked) to Present (✅) or Absent (❌), an instant alert is sent to your chat showing the subject, teacher, and status.

🗓️ Month Loop — Backdated Attendance Updates

Interval: Every 10 Mins

Teachers frequently enter or modify attendance records days after a class was conducted. QAttend periodically scans the university's full Monthly Register. If attendance is recorded retroactively for an earlier date, you receive an alert citing the exact historical class date and whether you were marked present or absent.

📚 Assignment Notifications

Interval: Every 30 Mins

QAttend checks your university academic portal for newly published assignments every 30 minutes. The notification provides the subject code, assignment topic, date published, and final submission deadline. Persistent fingerprint deduplication guarantees an assignment is never announced twice.

⏰ 7:00 PM IST — Assignment Deadline Reminders

Cron: 0 19 * * *

Every evening at 7:00 PM IST, QAttend reviews pending assignments. If an assignment deadline falls on today, a high-priority reminder is sent so you have time left to finalize your submission before the day concludes.

📊 On-Demand Attendance Summary

Manual Trigger

Whenever you want a complete, subject-by-subject attendance summary sent to your Telegram chat, click Send Attendance Summary on your web dashboard. Note: QAttend does not run an automated nightly summary spam job; summaries are triggered purely on-demand.

4. Automatic Chat Cleanliness (Auto-Delete)

To prevent your personal Telegram conversation from turning into an unreadable wall of text, QAttend features built-in automated message pruning:

  • Operational commands (such as date lookups and temporary queries) are automatically scheduled for deletion 60 seconds after delivery.
  • Status overview reports automatically delete after 2 minutes.
  • Important alerts (such as a Session Expired warning with a Reconnect button) are exempt from auto-delete and remain visible until you resolve them.

5. Disconnecting Telegram

You maintain full sovereignty over your Telegram channel. If you ever want to stop receiving notifications, click Disconnect in your dashboard or Telegram settings. QAttend instantly unlinks your chat ID from the database, and no further messages will be dispatched until you explicitly reconnect.
