How QAttend works

Track. Alert. Stay Updated.

Setup takes a few minutes. After that, QAttend keeps checking and notifying on its own.

1

Create your QAttend account

Register with your email and a password. Sign-in is handled through Firebase Authentication, and the password-reset flow also runs through it.

2

Connect QUMS

In QUMS Setup you enter your QID and QUMS password and solve the portal captcha. QAttend saves the credentials encrypted and keeps a separate session per account.

3

Connect Telegram

Open the Telegram page, press Connect Telegram, then press Start in the bot chat. That chat is now linked to your account — and to no one else's.

4

QAttend monitors your updates

QAttend periodically reads your attendance, timetable and assignments from QUMS and compares them with what it already knows, so it only alerts you about real changes.

5

Receive personalized notifications

Notifications are generated from your own data and sent to your own Telegram chat: today's classes each morning, attendance updates when marks are entered, assignment notices, deadline reminders, and a summary whenever you request one from the dashboard.

What runs in the background

  • Today's classes are sent each morning, before the first period.
  • Attendance is checked through college hours for newly marked periods.
  • The month register is scanned so later entries for earlier dates are caught.
  • New assignments are checked periodically.
  • A reminder goes out on the last date of an assignment.
Your data stays yours. Every account has its own QUMS session, attendance data, assignment state, watcher state and Telegram link. QAttend never sends one student's information to another student's chat.
