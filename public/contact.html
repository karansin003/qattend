Support Policies & SLA

Clear expectations for when you can expect a response.

QAttend is maintained as an independent student project. Support requests are handled directly by the lead developer with the following service standards:

  • Response Timeframe: Within 24 to 48 business hours for all email inquiries and submitted contact forms.
  • Operating Hours: Monday through Saturday, from 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM IST (Indian Standard Time).
  • Critical Outages: High-priority server incidents, portal changes, or widespread CAPTCHA relay issues are monitored continuously.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: If you wish to delete your QAttend account, email us or select "Account Deletion / Data Request" in the form above. We will purge your database record, session files, and Firebase credentials within 2 business days.

What to Include in Bug Reports

A little context makes technical problems much faster to resolve.

  • Which page or feature you were using (e.g. QUMS Setup, Dashboard, or Telegram Bot).
  • The exact error text or HTTP code shown on screen (e.g. "ConcurrentLoginError" or "Session Expired").
  • What you expected to happen versus what actually occurred.
  • Whether your QUMS connection and Telegram bot show as active in your account dashboard.
Security Precaution: Never share your QUMS password, personal security keys, or Telegram bot authentication tokens in messages. Support staff will never ask for your password.

Frequently Asked Support Questions

Quick answers to common questions before sending a message.

How do I reset my QAttend account password?

If you have forgotten your password, navigate to the Password Reset page, enter your registered email address, and submit the form. You will receive an official reset link powered by Firebase Authentication to securely choose a new password.

Why does my dashboard indicate "Session Expired"?

University portal cookies naturally expire after several days of inactivity or server maintenance. When this occurs, QAttend alerts you immediately. Simply click 🔐 Reconnect QUMS on your dashboard or in Telegram, solve the fresh visual CAPTCHA, and monitoring will instantly resume.

Can I link a different Telegram account or change devices?

Yes. You can unbind your active Telegram chat at any time by clicking Disconnect Telegram in your account dashboard. Afterwards, navigate to Telegram Setup to pair with a new chat ID or different Telegram account.

How can I request permanent deletion of all my data?

You maintain complete sovereignty over your information. To remove university credentials, click Reset / Delete QUMS Connection in QUMS Setup. To completely delete your user account, select "Account Deletion / Data Request" in the contact form above, and your entire database row and session files will be destroyed.
