1. Understanding the QUMS Connection Architecture

QUMS (Quantum University Management System) is the authoritative student portal where academic attendance, lecture timetables, and assignments are recorded.

QAttend operates as a secure companion application to the portal. Rather than storing your password in plain text or using fragile screen scraping, QAttend implements a robust, headless browser relay:

  • Headless Chromium Engine: When you initiate a connection, an isolated headless Chromium instance launches in the background and loads the official university login portal.
  • Visual Human CAPTCHA Relay: The portal requires a visual security CAPTCHA. QAttend captures a real-time screenshot of the official CAPTCHA and displays it directly to you.
  • No Automated Bypass: In strict compliance with security standards, QAttend never attempts to crack, OCR, or bypass the university's CAPTCHA. The student solves the challenge personally.
  • Encrypted Session Storage: Upon successful login, the authenticated session cookies are saved to an isolated session file on disk with strict 0600 (owner-only) permissions, and your password is encrypted using AES-256-GCM.

2. Step-by-Step Connection Walkthrough

1

Open QUMS Setup

From your QAttend dashboard, navigate to QUMS Setup. Make sure you are signed in to your QAttend account.

2

Enter QID and University Password

Enter your student Registration ID (QID) and your current university portal password. Double-check for typos.

3

Fetch and Solve the CAPTCHA

Click Get Captcha. Within 2–4 seconds, the portal's security image will load. Type the exact characters displayed in the box.

4

Submit and Confirm

Click Submit Captcha & Login. Once authenticated, your subjects and timetable will load onto your dashboard automatically.

3. Troubleshooting Common Login & Session Issues

Issue 1: "Another QUMS reconnect or login is already in progress" (429 Error)

Cause: To ensure server stability and avoid triggering rate limits on the university network, QAttend enforces a strict concurrency limit of 1 active browser launch per user at a time.

Solution: Wait approximately 20 to 30 seconds for the existing background process to conclude or automatically terminate, then click "Get Captcha" once more.

Issue 2: Expired CAPTCHA / Timeout

Cause: The university portal invalidates pending CAPTCHA challenges after 5 minutes (PENDING_TTL_MS = 5 * 60 * 1000). If you leave the setup screen open without submitting, the token expires.

Solution: Do not pause on the CAPTCHA screen. If you were delayed, click Reload Captcha to refresh the session token before submitting.

Issue 3: Ambiguous or Distorted Characters

Cause: The university CAPTCHA generator occasionally produces overlapping letters or ambiguous glyphs (such as uppercase 'O' versus numeral '0', or lowercase 'l' versus numeral '1').

Solution: If any character looks unclear, do not guess. Click Reload Captcha to generate a cleaner, more legible verification image immediately.

Issue 4: Periodic Session Expiry ("SessionExpiredError")

Cause: University portal sessions are not permanent; the university's web servers expire session cookies after multi-day inactivity, system maintenance, or portal reboots.

Solution: When your session expires, QAttend detects the rejection during background checks and sends a notification with a Reconnect button. Because your QID and encrypted password remain saved, clicking "Reconnect" auto-fills your credentials—you only need to enter the visual CAPTCHA to restore your active tracking!

Issue 5: Password Changed on the University Portal

Cause: If you reset or updated your password directly on the university portal, QAttend's stored encrypted password will fail to authenticate.

Solution: On the QUMS Setup page, click Reset / Delete QUMS Connection. This completely wipes the stored credentials from the database. Then, simply connect again using your new password.

4. Security Best Practices

  • Never share your password: No administrator or support personnel from QAttend will ever request your university password.
  • No password exposure: Your password is never rendered on dashboard HTML or sent in Telegram messages.
  • Session isolation: Every registered student maintains a dedicated session file and unique encryption initialization vector.
  • Permanent Reset Control: You maintain full authority to delete your stored QUMS connection at any time using the one-click reset control.
