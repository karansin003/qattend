What QAttend is

QAttend is an attendance tracking and notification platform for students who use the QUMS portal. It links to a student's own QUMS account, keeps an eye on their attendance, timetable and assignments, and sends the important parts to that student's own Telegram chat.

It is a companion to the portal, not a replacement. The portal remains the source of truth; QAttend simply watches it for changes and tells you about them.

The problem it solves

Attendance in the portal is updated at the teacher's convenience. A class you attended in the morning may be marked in the afternoon, and a class from last week may be corrected later. Assignment lists are similar — new tasks appear without a notification.

  • Students have to keep logging in to check whether anything changed.
  • Changes made after the fact are easy to miss entirely.
  • Add-drop decisions and the 75% requirement are hard to reason about without current numbers.

Why it was created

QAttend started as a practical answer to a repeated problem: checking attendance manually, several times a day, is slow and unreliable. Instead of refreshing a portal, a student should be able to connect their account once and be told when something actually changes.

It is built and maintained as an independent student project using the same tooling that runs it in production today: Node.js, Express, Playwright, PostgreSQL (or a JSON fallback in local development) and the Telegram Bot API.

Core product principles

  • Facts over hype. Every feature listed on this site is a feature that actually runs.
  • Privacy by design. QUMS credentials are stored encrypted and are never displayed back.
  • Isolation by default. Each account has its own session, data and Telegram link.
  • No noise. Change detection and de-duplication keep alerts meaningful.
  • You stay in control. You choose when to connect, reconnect or disconnect services.
  • Small and dependable. A lightweight frontend and a focused backend that is easy to reason about.
note QAttend is an independent project. It is not an official QUMS or university service, and it does not claim any certification, partnership or affiliation. Always treat the portal as the authoritative record of your attendance.
