1. Attendance Intelligence & Exam Eligibility

University examination regulations enforce a strict 75% attendance threshold across all registered courses. QAttend transforms raw portal numbers into clear mathematical clarity so you always know where you stand.

When students check university portals manually, they are presented with isolated tables of attended versus held classes. What is frequently missing is the forward-looking calculation: "If I fall sick today, will I still be permitted to sit for my end-term examinations?" or "How many consecutive classes must I attend without missing a single one to reach 75% again?"

QAttend answers these questions continuously through automated parsing and exact mathematical algorithms directly aligned with official university metrics.

📊

Subject-by-Subject Tracking

QAttend extracts your authenticated attendance records from the QUMS portal, categorizing data by subject name, course code, attended lectures, and total classes held. It reports current percentages alongside your official portal overall percentage.

🎯

75% Recovery Guidance

If a subject falls below the required 75% threshold, QAttend computes the exact number of future consecutive lectures you must attend using the formula k = Math.ceil((0.75 * Total - Attended) / 0.25).

🛡️

Safe Margin / Skippable Count

When attendance is healthy (≥ 75%), QAttend computes your skippable buffer using k = Math.floor((Attended - 0.75 * Total) / 0.75), telling you precisely how many classes you can afford to miss without falling below the regulatory limit.

2. Real-Time Telegram Automation

Automated academic notifications delivered straight to your personal Telegram conversation—no more manually refreshing web portals.

Students typically check their attendance portal multiple times per day because lecture marks are submitted unpredictably. Faculty members frequently log attendance hours after the lecture has ended, or in late evening batches. QAttend takes over this repetitive monitoring through dedicated, asynchronous background workers.

🌅

8:30 AM Daily Timetable

Every morning at 8:30 AM IST (before 9:00 AM lectures commence), QAttend pushes your complete daily schedule to Telegram with classroom numbers, period timings, and assigned faculty members.

⚡

Real-Time Period Alerts

During college hours (08:30–17:00 IST), our fast polling loop checks your timetable every 5 minutes. As soon as a professor marks attendance, an instant alert arrives with the course name, teacher, and status (✅ Present / ❌ Absent).

🗓️

Backdated Month Register Scans

When faculty update records retroactively for earlier dates, standard daily checks miss them. QAttend scans the university's full Month Register every 10 minutes to notify you of retroactive modifications with the exact historical class date.

3. Academic Deadlines & Interactive Bot Commands

Never lose track of assignments, practical submissions, or historical academic records.

University coursework often carries tight submission deadlines. Instructors upload homework assignments, laboratory files, and project requirements to the portal without broadcasting push notifications. QAttend bridges this gap by discovering newly posted assignments and delivering timely evening reminders.

📚

New Assignment Discovery

QAttend polls the university assignment API every 30 minutes. Newly published assignments, quizzes, and practical write-ups are immediately dispatched to your Telegram chat with topic details and deadlines.

⏰

7:00 PM Deadline Warnings

Every evening at 7:00 PM IST, QAttend reviews upcoming assignment deadlines. If an assignment is due today, you receive an urgent reminder so you have sufficient time to complete and submit it.

💬

Historical Date Lookups

Send any date in DD/MM/YYYY format (e.g., 14/10/2026) directly to the Telegram bot to inspect your exact attendance breakdown for that calendar date. Responses auto-delete after 60 seconds to maintain chat cleanliness.

4. Security & Student Data Privacy

Architectural and engineering practices designed specifically to safeguard student credentials and academic data.

Handling university portal credentials demands rigorous cryptographic standards. QAttend was built with privacy by design: your credentials are never exposed in readable text, never displayed on public pages, and never shared across student accounts.

🔐

AES-256-GCM Encryption

University passwords stored for session recovery are encrypted using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with unique initialization vectors and SHA-256/scrypt key derivation.

👥

Strict Multi-User Isolation

Each student account maintains an isolated session file (mode 0600), separate database rows, and a unique Telegram chat ID. Your academic data is never cross-shared or pooled.

🗑️

Permanent Reset Control

You maintain total authority over your data. A single click on Reset / Delete QUMS Connection permanently destroys saved university credentials and deletes active session files from disk.
