Features

Track. Alert. Stay Updated.

Every feature listed here is part of the working QAttend application today. Nothing is listed that the product does not do.

Attendance Tracking

QAttend reads your attendance from the QUMS portal and shows it subject by subject: subject name and code, attended classes, total classes, absent count and the current percentage.

🎯

75% Requirement Guidance

For each subject QAttend shows whether you are above or below the 75% requirement, how many classes you still need to reach it, and how many you can miss while staying safe.

🌅

Daily Class Notifications

Each morning your classes for that day are sent to your Telegram with the period timing, room and teacher, so you start the day knowing exactly where to be.

⚡

Telegram Alerts

When a teacher marks your attendance, QAttend detects the change and sends you a Telegram update with the subject, period, teacher and whether you were marked present or absent.

🗓️

Backdated Attendance Detection

Attendance is often entered after the class date. QAttend also reads the month register, so marks added later for an earlier date still reach you — with the real class date in the message.

Notifications and academic updates

📚

Assignment Notifications

QAttend checks your QUMS assignment list for newly published assignments and informs you on Telegram, so a new task does not go unnoticed.

⏰

Assignment Deadline Reminder

On the last date of an assignment you receive a reminder, so you still have time left to submit it.

🌙

Attendance Summary

The dashboard has a manual Send Attendance Summary button. It builds your current attendance report and sends it to your own Telegram chat immediately. There is no automatic nightly summary.

🏠

Student Dashboard

A personal dashboard that greets you by your student name and shows attendance, QUMS connection status, Telegram connection status and quick actions in one place.

🔗

QUMS Integration

QUMS Setup connects your account through the portal's own captcha login. QAttend stores the credentials encrypted, keeps the session separate for every user, and can reconnect when the session expires.

🔐

Per-Student Isolation

Every account has its own QUMS session, attendance data, assignment state and Telegram link. One student's updates are never delivered to another student's chat.
