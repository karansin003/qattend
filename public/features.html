Attendance Intelligence & Analysis

Transform raw university records into actionable academic insight.

📊

Subject-by-Subject Tracking

QAttend extracts your authenticated attendance table from the QUMS portal, categorizing records by subject name, course code, attended lectures, and total classes held. It shows current percentages alongside your official portal overall percentage.

🎯

75% Recovery Guidance

If a subject falls below the required 75% examination threshold, QAttend calculates the exact number of consecutive future lectures you must attend using the formula Math.ceil((0.75 * Total - Attended) / 0.25).

Read Calculator Guide →

🛡️

Safe Bunk / Margin Margin

When attendance is healthy (≥ 75%), QAttend computes your skippable buffer using Math.floor((Attended - 0.75 * Total) / 0.75), telling you precisely how many classes you can afford to miss without falling below the regulatory limit.

Real-Time Telegram Automation

Automated notifications delivered directly to your personal Telegram conversation.

🌅

8:30 AM Daily Timetable

Every morning at 8:30 AM IST (before 9:00 AM lectures begin), QAttend pushes your complete daily schedule to Telegram with room numbers, period timings, and assigned faculty members.

⚡

Real-Time Period Alerts

During college hours (08:30–17:00 IST), our fast polling loop checks your timetable every 5 minutes. As soon as a professor marks attendance, an instant alert arrives with the course name, teacher, and status (✅ Present / ❌ Absent).

🗓️

Backdated Month Register Scans

When faculty update records retroactively for earlier dates, standard daily checks miss them. QAttend scans the university's full Month Register every 10 minutes to notify you of retroactive modifications with the exact historical class date.

Academic Deadlines & Bot Commands

Never lose track of assignments, due dates, or historical records.

📚

New Assignment Discovery

QAttend polls the university assignment API every 30 minutes. Newly published assignments, quizzes, and practical write-ups are immediately dispatched to your Telegram chat with topic details and deadlines.

⏰

7:00 PM Deadline Warnings

Every evening at 7:00 PM IST, QAttend reviews upcoming assignment deadlines. If an assignment is due today, you receive an urgent reminder so you have sufficient time to complete and submit it.

💬

Historical Date Lookups

Send any date in DD/MM/YYYY format (e.g., 14/10/2026) directly to the Telegram bot to inspect your exact attendance breakdown for that calendar date. Responses auto-delete after 60 seconds to maintain chat cleanliness.

Security & Student Privacy

Engineering practices designed to safeguard student accounts.

🔐

AES-256-GCM Encryption

University passwords stored for session recovery are encrypted using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with unique initialization vectors and SHA-256/scrypt key derivation.

👥

Strict Multi-User Isolation

Each student account maintains an isolated session file (mode 0600), separate database rows, and a unique Telegram chat ID. Your academic data is never cross-shared or pooled.

🗑️

Permanent Reset Control

You maintain total authority over your data. A single click on Reset / Delete QUMS Connection permanently destroys saved university credentials and deletes active session files from disk.
