Frequently asked questions

Short, accurate answers about how QAttend behaves. No feature is described here unless it exists.

What is QAttend?

QAttend is a student attendance tracking and notification platform integrated with QUMS and Telegram. It connects to your QUMS account, tracks your attendance and academic updates, and sends personalized notifications to your own Telegram chat.

How does QAttend work?

You create an account, connect QUMS once (QID, password and portal captcha), and connect Telegram. After that, QAttend periodically reads your attendance, timetable and assignments, notices changes, and sends you a message when there is something worth telling you about.

What is QUMS?

QUMS is the university management portal where your academic records live — including your attendance, timetable and assignments. QAttend acts as a companion to that portal: it reads your data from it and does not replace it.

How do I connect QUMS?

Open QUMS Setup from the dashboard. Enter your QID and QUMS password, press Get Captcha, then type the captcha text shown in the image and submit. On success your connection is saved. If the session later expires, use Reconnect QUMS — it usually only needs the captcha again.

How do I connect Telegram?

Open the Telegram page and press Connect Telegram. The bot chat opens; press Start there. QAttend then links that chat to your account and confirms it. If you were already connected, it will tell you that you are already connected.

Will my attendance updates be sent to someone else?

No. Notifications are sent only to the Telegram chat linked to your own account, and they are built from your own data. Each account keeps a separate QUMS session, attendance data, assignment state and Telegram link.

How does backdated attendance detection work?

Besides checking today's periods, QAttend also scans the month register for your classes. If attendance is entered later for an earlier class date, you receive an alert that shows the actual class date and whether you were marked present or absent (including multiple lectures on the same day).

How are assignment notifications handled?

QAttend reads your assignment list from QUMS periodically and compares it with what it has already seen. When a new assignment appears, you get a Telegram notification. The same assignment is not announced twice.

When are assignment deadline reminders sent?

A reminder is sent on the last date of an assignment, so you still have time to submit before the deadline passes.

Can I disconnect Telegram?

Yes. On the dashboard (and on the Telegram page) use Disconnect. Your chat is then unlinked and no further messages are sent there until you connect again.

Can I logout?

Yes. Logout in the menu ends your QAttend session on that browser. You can also log out of QUMS itself from the dashboard, which deletes the stored QUMS session; reconnecting after that normally only requires the captcha again.

Is my QUMS password displayed publicly?

No. Your QUMS password is stored encrypted and is never displayed back to you, never shown on any QAttend page, and never included in a Telegram message. Your QAttend account password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is not stored in plain text.

Does QAttend send an automatic attendance summary every night?

No. The attendance summary is manual: you press Send Attendance Summary on the dashboard and the current report is generated and sent to your Telegram immediately. Scheduled notifications cover today's classes, attendance changes, new assignments and deadline reminders.

What happens if my QUMS session expires?

QAttend tells you that the session needs attention. Open QUMS Setup and reconnect: because your credentials are already saved encrypted, you normally only need to solve the captcha again.

Do I need to keep the QAttend website open?

No. Once QUMS and Telegram are connected, the background checks and notifications run on the server. You can close the browser and still receive messages.
