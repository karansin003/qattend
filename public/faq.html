Attendance Tracking & Calculations

How does QAttend calculate my attendance percentage?

QAttend reads your attended and total class counts directly from the QUMS academic portal. The percentage is calculated as (Attended Classes / Total Classes Conducted) * 100. For overall semester attendance, QAttend directly prioritizes the university portal's official overall percentage field (st.TotalPercentage) to ensure exact fidelity with the examination records.

How are "Classes Needed to Reach 75%" calculated?

When a subject is below 75%, every future attended lecture adds 1 to both your attended count and total count: (Attended + k) / (Total + k) >= 0.75. Solving for k yields k = Math.ceil((0.75 * Total - Attended) / 0.25). For detailed worked examples and an interactive tool, visit our Attendance Calculator Guide.

How does the "Margin / Skippable Classes" calculation work?

When your attendance is at or above 75%, QAttend calculates how many classes you could miss consecutively without dropping below the threshold: k = Math.floor((Attended - 0.75 * Total) / 0.75). If your attendance is exactly 75% or very close, the margin is 0, indicating any missed class will drop you below the threshold.

Why didn't my attendance percentage change immediately after my class?

QAttend reflects data as it is recorded on the university portal. Faculty members typically submit attendance records later in the afternoon, in evening batches, or sometimes days later. QAttend cannot display an attendance mark before the teacher submits it to the portal.

QUMS Connection & Authentication

Why do I have to solve a CAPTCHA when connecting QUMS?

The university portal requires a visual CAPTCHA verification challenge during student login to prevent automated bot abuse. In compliance with security standards, QAttend never attempts to bypass or OCR solve the CAPTCHA; a headless browser on our server loads the challenge and relays the real image to your screen for you to solve personally.

What does "ConcurrentLoginError" or 429 status mean?

To maintain system stability and avoid triggering rate limits on university infrastructure, QAttend allows only 1 active background browser launch per user at a time. If you click "Get Captcha" multiple times in quick succession, wait 20–30 seconds for the existing process to conclude, then click once.

Why did my QUMS session expire, and how do I reconnect?

University portal servers automatically expire active login sessions after multi-day inactivity, weekly maintenance reboots, or cookie timeouts. When this happens, QAttend alerts you with a Reconnect button. Because your QID and encrypted password remain saved, reconnecting does not require re-typing your credentials—you only need to enter the visual CAPTCHA code.

What should I do if I change my password on the university portal?

If you updated your password on the university site, QAttend's stored encrypted password will no longer work. Navigate to QUMS Setup, click Reset / Delete QUMS Connection to permanently erase the old credentials, and then connect using your new password.

Telegram Alerts & Schedules

What notifications does QAttend actually send to Telegram?

QAttend delivers: (1) Daily class timetable every morning at 8:30 AM IST; (2) Real-time attendance alerts during college hours (08:30–17:00 IST) when a professor marks a lecture; (3) Backdated attendance alerts when records are modified retroactively; (4) New assignment notifications every 30 minutes; (5) Urgent assignment deadline reminders at 7:00 PM IST for assignments due today; and (6) On-demand attendance summaries whenever requested.

Does QAttend send an automated summary every night at 9:00 PM?

No. QAttend does not operate an automated nightly summary job. Daily summaries are strictly manual and on-demand: clicking "Send Attendance Summary" on your dashboard generates and dispatches the summary immediately to your chat.

How does backdated attendance detection work?

In addition to monitoring today's active classes, QAttend periodically scans the university's full Month Register. If an instructor marks or amends attendance for an earlier date, QAttend detects the difference, cites the exact historical class date, and sends an alert.

Can I look up attendance for a past date on Telegram?

Yes! Send any date in DD/MM/YYYY or DD/MM/YY format (for example: 14/10/2026) directly to the Telegram bot. It will look up your authenticated records and return a complete period-by-period breakdown for that day.

Why do some Telegram bot messages disappear after 1 or 2 minutes?

QAttend features an automated message pruning system to keep your personal chat clutter-free. Interactive commands and date queries automatically delete after 60 seconds, while /status reports delete after 2 minutes. Important alerts (such as Session Expired notices) remain until resolved.

Can I disconnect Telegram at any time?

Yes. Clicking Disconnect in your dashboard or Telegram settings immediately unlinks your chat ID. No further alerts or summaries will be sent until you choose to reconnect.

Security, Data Isolation & Privacy

Is my QUMS university password visible to anyone?

No. Your university password is encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being stored in the database. It is never displayed on dashboard pages, never returned in API payloads, and never sent across Telegram.

Will my academic records ever be sent to another student?

No. QAttend enforces strict multi-tenant isolation. Every account maintains a private session file, an isolated database record, and an exclusive Telegram chat binding. Notifications address you by your official student name from QUMS—never your email.

How can I delete my data or remove my university connection?

You have full control: clicking Reset / Delete QUMS Connection immediately destroys stored credentials and removes active session files from the server. To delete your entire user account, submit a request via our Contact page.

Is QAttend officially affiliated with Quantum University or QUMS?

No. QAttend is an independent student project created to assist students with timely academic notifications. It is not an official university service and does not claim any certification or institutional affiliation. The official university portal remains the authoritative source of truth.
